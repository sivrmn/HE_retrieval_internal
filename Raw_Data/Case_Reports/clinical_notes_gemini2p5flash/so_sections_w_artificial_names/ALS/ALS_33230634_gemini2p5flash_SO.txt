S (Subjective)
The patient is a 56-year-old previously healthy male presenting with a history of progressive, widespread muscle weakness and lower back pain. He reports a 1-year history of persistent lower back pain, which was initially managed with NSAIDs for 8 months without relief. Following this, he underwent a traditional medicinal procedure known as Ogosaraka in his native Kisii region of Kenya, which involved scarification of the lower back and insertion of a black metal powder into the incisions.

Approximately 4 months after this procedure, he developed progressive muscle weakness in his legs, arms, and neck, leading to a "head drop" and difficulty walking and carrying objects. He also reports difficulty with coughing and has experienced significant weight loss. The patient reports no improvement in his lower back pain since the procedure. He has since traveled to Norway to seek further medical care.
