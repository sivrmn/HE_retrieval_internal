Clinical Note
Patient Name: Neolyx Bitris

S (Subjective)
Neolyx Bitris is a 16-year-old female who reports a two-month history of progressively worsening restricted movement in her elbows and shoulders. The limitation has become so severe that she is now unable to feed or dress herself. She reports she is still able to walk independently at home, though with great difficulty. She was born with a bilateral great toe deformity. A bump on her right scapula was first noted at age 7, followed by the development of multiple bony swellings throughout her body over time. She denies any other symptoms at this time. She attained menarche at age 14.

O (Objective)
Vitals: Not specified.

Physical Examination:

Shoulders: Right shoulder has a fixed abduction deformity of 30º; left shoulder has a fixed abduction deformity of 10º. Right shoulder flexion-extension is limited to 10º with very limited rotation.

Elbows: Right elbow is fixed in 70º of flexion. Left elbow has a 15º flexion deformity. Bilateral restriction of pronation-supination.

Wrists: Right wrist has a 20º dorsiflexion deformity. Left wrist is fixed in 30º of volar flexion.

Hips: Right hip is fixed in 70º of flexion. Left hip movement is restricted.

Cervical Spine: Restricted movement of 20º each for forward flexion, lateral flexion, and rotation.

Trunk: Rigid kyphosis with torticollis. Prominent trapezius and scalene muscles noted. Chest excursion is 2.8 cm.
