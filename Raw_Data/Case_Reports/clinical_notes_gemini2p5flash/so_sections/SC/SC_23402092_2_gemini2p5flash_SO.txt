She was recently referred for management of her poorly controlled disease. She also reports a recent history of an ischiorectal abscess that was drained.

Labs/Imaging:

HIV: Negative

Tuberculosis (TB): Excluded

HLA-B27: Positive

Liver Function Tests (LFTs): Persistently elevated ALP and γGT.

Magnetic Resonance Cholangiography (MRC): Smooth intra- and extra-hepatic bile ducts, normal gallbladder, no biliary obstruction. CBD measured 10 mm.
