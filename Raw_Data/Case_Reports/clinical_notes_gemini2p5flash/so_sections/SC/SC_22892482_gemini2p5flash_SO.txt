Patient: Arthur Chen
Date: August 22, 2025

S: Subjective
The patient is a 33-year-old male with a 12-year history of Crohn's disease (CD) who presents with a one-year history of worsening abdominal pain, bloody diarrhea, and jaundice. He reports an exacerbation of his CD symptoms despite being on maintenance mesalamine. Over the last year, he has experienced intermittent fever, rectal hemorrhage, and a 5 kg weight loss. He also reports painless jaundice and pruritus. The patient notes a one-week history of pain in his knees and elbows. His previous CD management included sulfasalazine, steroids, and metronidazole.

O: Objective
Vitals: Not provided.

Infectious Workup: Negative for viral hepatitis (A, B, C, CMV, EB, HS, VZ) and stool pathogens (shigella, salmonella, Vibrio cholerae, Mycobacterium tuberculosis, halophilic bacteria, and proteus).
