Subjective

Chief Complaint: A 73-year-old female presents with a 10-year history of right-sided facial pain.

History of Present Illness: Pain is described as chronic and has not responded to previous medication management.

Objective

Imaging: Magnetic resonance angiography (MRA) showed a persistent primitive trigeminal artery variant (PPTAv) on the right side, compressing the trigeminal nerve. The artery was noted to originate from the right internal carotid artery.

Intraoperative Findings (Microvascular Decompression - MVD): The offending vessel was identified as a PPTAv. It was observed to continue posteriorly, passing through Meckel's cavity and becoming the anterior inferior cerebellar artery.

Postoperative Imaging: Computed tomography angiography (CTA) confirmed the intraoperative findings, showing the PPTAv continuing as the anterior inferior cerebellar artery and supplying the cerebellar hemisphere.
